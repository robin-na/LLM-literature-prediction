## Main Findings Relevant To Prediction
The paper finds that the ability of consumers to punish a monopolist (e.g., via boycott) and thereby reduce prices depends on the number of consumers and the degree of solidarity among them. With few consumers, individual punishment can be effective and lead to lower prices. As the number of consumers increases, the incentive to individually punish diminishes (public good problem), and the monopolist can more easily charge higher (Cournot) prices unless solidarity is high. The model predicts that successful collective punishment (boycott) is more likely in small groups or when solidarity is strong, and less likely in large, uncoordinated groups.

## Prediction Guidance
This paper provides theoretical insight into how group size and solidarity affect the effectiveness of punishment in public-good-like environments, specifically in the context of consumer boycotts against monopolists. While it does not provide quantitative predictions of efficiency, it suggests that increasing group size without mechanisms for solidarity or coordination will reduce the effectiveness of punishment in improving group outcomes. For prediction tasks, this implies that punishment is less likely to improve efficiency in large, uncoordinated groups, but may be effective in small groups or when solidarity is high. However, the lack of direct efficiency or payoff data limits its quantitative predictive value.

## Important Limitations
- The paper is theoretical and does not report new empirical data or direct measures of efficiency or group payoff.
- The model is static and does not address repeated interactions or learning dynamics.
- Punishment is modeled as a boycott (rejection of purchase), which is analogous but not identical to punishment in standard public goods games.
- Key parameters such as punishment cost and effectiveness are not parameterized as in standard PGGs, limiting direct comparability.
- Empirical support is referenced from other studies, but the paper does not provide new experimental results.
