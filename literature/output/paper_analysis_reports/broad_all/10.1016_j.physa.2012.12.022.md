Outcome notes:
The main reported outcome is the fraction of cooperators (cooperation rate) as a function of model parameters. The paper discusses payoffs in the model description and uses them to define aspiration levels, but does not report group payoff, efficiency, or total earnings as a primary outcome. The analysis is focused on cooperation dynamics, not on efficiency or welfare.

## Main Findings Relevant To Prediction
The study finds that introducing conditional participation (interpreted as a form of punishment) in a spatial public goods game can promote cooperation, but only within an optimal range of aspiration levels. If aspiration is too low, the effect is minimal; if too high, cooperation is suppressed. The transition from defection to cooperation can be abrupt or gradual depending on aspiration and synergy factor. The main reported outcome is the fraction of cooperators, not group efficiency or payoff. The model does not include standard costly punishment or direct payoff reduction, and does not report efficiency or welfare outcomes.

## Prediction Guidance
This paper is of limited direct use for predicting the effect of enabling standard punishment on efficiency in public goods games, as it does not study standard punishment mechanisms (e.g., costly punishment with direct payoff reduction) and does not report efficiency or group payoff outcomes. The conditional participation mechanism is adjacent to punishment, but differs in implementation and effect. The main outcome is cooperation rate, not efficiency. The findings may be useful for understanding how conditional participation or abstention can affect cooperation, but should not be directly used to predict efficiency effects of standard punishment interventions.

## Design Dimensions Highlighted In This Paper
- player count: The paper directly informs this dimension. Each group consists of 5 players (N=5), and the lattice size is varied (e.g., 100x100), but the group size is always 5.
- number of rounds: The paper directly informs this dimension. Simulations run for 30,000 time steps (rounds), with outcomes averaged over the last 2,000 steps.
- all-or-nothing contribution: The paper directly informs this dimension. Contribution is binary: each player either contributes 1 (cooperator) or 0 (defector) per game.
- mpcr: The paper directly informs this dimension. Synergy factor r is varied (e.g., r=3.5, 4, 4.5, etc.), with MPCR = r/N (N=5).

## Important Limitations
- The paper does not study standard costly punishment mechanisms; conditional participation is only adjacent to punishment.
- No efficiency, group payoff, or welfare outcomes are reported; only cooperation rate is analyzed.
- Findings are based on theoretical modeling and simulation, not empirical or experimental data.
- The model outcome (fraction of cooperators) is not directly translatable to efficiency or payoff-based outcomes.
- The conditional participation mechanism is not equivalent to enabling punishment in standard public goods games.
